# مواصفة المرحلة الثالثة: الإدارة الأكاديمية وحساب الطالب المالي

## Problem Statement

The completed local pilot gives a المركز an isolated workspace, branches, staff membership, branch assignments, authentication, and audit. Its administration interface does not yet support the daily academic journey: maintaining students and instructors, setting up independent branch curricula, enrolling students, scheduling and closing lectures, reviewing absence, transferring students, arranging make-ups, or deciding study completion.

Staff also need an actual حساب الطالب المالي to preserve fees, payments, available credit, and debt during enrollment and academic moves. A displayed balance without traceable transactions would not support those decisions. Attendance, coverage, teaching time, and financial movements must retain their distinct meanings and histories.

## Solution

Extend the existing center administration workspace into a complete local operating journey for academic administration and the simple student financial account. Use the root domain glossary and ADRs 0001–0026, particularly the final decisions on whole numbered lectures, the approved lecture count for each group, and the Phase 3 boundary.

Students and instructors have one profile per center. Each branch has an independent Course → Stage → Level → Group → Session sequence. Study attempts preserve enrollment, primary-group history, attendance, content coverage, waiting, transfers, and make-ups. Staff record actual instructors and their teaching durations separately from student attendance. The eligibility report uses whole required lectures, with explicit content equivalence and no duplicate credit. Open-session attendance contributes a clearly marked provisional percentage. Staff review eligible students and problems when completing a group and explicitly select whose study completion to approve; the approval waits until sessions supporting the calculation are closed. The report remains available for later make-ups and approval without reopening the group.

Financial actions explicitly record fees, payments, allocations, settlements, refunds, and corrections in one student account per center, with branch-scoped visibility and approval. Academic moves do not silently create financial actions. Existing tenancy, authorization, query-budget, audit, Arabic RTL, and shared-interface contracts remain acceptance requirements.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Authority and scope:** ADRs 0001–0026 and the root glossary are the domain authority. ADRs 0023 and 0024 replace the older hours-weighted and split-lecture proposals. ADR 0025 fixes this phase's boundary. Existing pilot functionality is a foundation, not proof of academic implementation.
- **Persistence boundary:** Extend the per-center data model and center migration lifecycle for student and instructor profiles, branch academic structures, plan versions, groups, assignments, study attempts, dated branch/level/plan contexts and primary-group associations, waiting history, sessions, actual teaching records, attendance, make-ups, equivalences, requirement changes, completion decisions, and financial records. Keep login identities and basic center memberships central. Do not join central and tenant databases or merge profiles across centers.
- **Profile access:** Basic student search has a center-level opt-in, disabled by default. Enabling it does not grant academic or financial access outside assigned branches. Duplicate warnings themselves must not reveal unauthorized profiles. Instructor files and teaching assignments remain distinct from login accounts.
- **Authorization:** Extend the existing permission evaluation and branch assignments with fixed combinable operational roles and an explicit action matrix. Registration handles profiles, enrollment, waiting, and moves; attendance handles session recording and closure; academic administration handles plans, groups, equivalence, closed-attendance correction, and revocation; accounting handles ordinary financial entries and payments, while registration applies the approved price and any authorized discount as part of enrollment. A separate fee-discount permission can be granted to registration staff and requires a reason, including at 100 percent. Sensitive refunds and settlements require additional financial approval; continuing after a debt warning does not. Preserve owner and center-administrator authority and existing branch roles; generic branch read/update permission must not silently substitute for sensitive operational permission.
- **Curriculum:** Branch structures are independent. Cross-branch copy is a one-time operation subject to source and destination authorization. Groups retain their plan version; attempts retain the effective version and requirements for each dated context rather than overwriting their history during transfers. Academic requirement changes create versions or explicit audited group amendments with impact previews; no ordinary edit mutates current requirements invisibly.
- **Whole lectures:** Use required whole numbered lectures with optional titles and independent planned-hour data. Each actual session is numbered within its group. An approved additional teaching day is a whole added requirement; do not represent one required lecture as fractional attendance across days. Preserve the distinction between a replacement and an additional requirement.
- **Primary enrollment:** Enforce one active primary-group association per attempt, with dated history. Waiting belongs to the level and attempt, not a synthetic group. Reattachment and an approved transfer to an equivalent level in another branch preserve the same attempt; restarting creates a new one. Keep prior branch, level, plan, and primary-group associations as dated history, with at most one current study context and one current primary group. Attendance and money remain attributed to their original sources; reading the current attempt does not grant access to every prior branch record. The relational table sketch discussed with the user is approved in principle only, not a fixed physical schema. Determine attendance eligibility from association dates. A transfer changes neither fees nor payment allocations automatically.
- **Transfer and version previews:** Show destination version and approved count, credited full requirements through valid equivalence, missing content, and any debt warning and available-credit suggestion before confirmation. The authorized applicant may proceed despite debt; spending existing credit remains a separate permitted financial action. Preserve previous calculations and decisions. Recheck authorization, versions, and eligibility when executing a previewed action.
- **Sessions and actual teaching:** Support recurring-date preview and individual sessions, postponement, unheld-session cancellation, and distinct revocation of held-session approval. Store each actual instructor's teaching duration, including substitutes and simultaneous participation; these records do not calculate instructor pay.
- **Attendance:** An eligible unrecorded student remains unrecorded until closure. Closure records absence only for eligible unrecorded students of a held session. Counted attendance grants complete applicable requirements; open-session contributions are provisional in the report until closure. No partial attendance, per-student content fragments, or student-minute weighting. A canceled session is not an absence.
- **Corrections:** Closed-session attendance correction requires academic authority, a reason, and before/after history. Undo is restricted to the employee's own latest unchanged entry in an open session. Recompute dependent coverage and absence after corrections or approval revocation, without silently reversing a manual enrollment move or rewriting a previously approved completion decision.
- **Make-ups and equivalence:** Keep a make-up link to the attempt and existing destination session, separate from primary enrollment. A reservation gives no credit. Identical required content in the same plan version is directly creditable; other-plan substitution needs approved equivalence and its reason/approver. Credit whole requirements at most once, retain original absences, and respect closed-session correction controls for retrospective entry. Display حضور تعويض rather than an ambiguous guest label.
- **Absence review:** Resolve group → level → stage → course settings, defaulting to consecutive absence and supporting total absence and explicit disabling. Count finalized actual absences only. Recalculate after rule changes and corrections. Reset the operating counter upon reattachment after waiting, preserving historical absences. Reports support authorized branch/course/stage/level scopes. Bulk movement previews exact names and count, then rechecks current eligibility and explains skips; reaching a threshold alone never moves anyone.
- **Completion:** Divide distinct fulfilled required lectures by the group's approved count. Apply the inherited threshold with a group override using whole-count comparison; 80% of eleven requires nine. Late-join regularity is distinct from total coverage. New thresholds affect new registrations by default; current registrations require explicit application and impact preview. Retain approved completion decisions and dates. Show the eligibility report during study, including qualifying students, missing content, and review problems. Count open-session attendance immediately in a clearly provisional percentage with a warning. When changing the group to completed, academic staff select the students whose study completion they approve; approval waits until sessions supporting the calculation are closed and current eligibility is rechecked. Reaching the threshold never automatically approves completion. Keep the review report available for later make-ups and student approval without reopening the group. Record exceptional completion separately with reason, true coverage, and missing content. Completing the group alone does not reduce its approved required count; an explicit approved requirement change follows the existing cancellation/amendment rules. Course completion still requires every required level independently. Upsell and follow-up actions remain deferred.
- **Cancellation requirements:** Replacement preserves the approved count and substitutes the canceled requirement without duplicate credit. Final cancellation without replacement requires approval and preview before reducing the count. Academic and financial compensation are independent explicit decisions. Requirement amendments preserve history and previously approved completion decisions.
- **Financial model:** Maintain one account per student per center and branch-attributed source movements, with registration attribution when applicable. At registration, snapshot the approved original study-attempt price, any authorized discount and mandatory discount reason, net fee, and actor. Permit a discount through 100 percent but never above the price or into negative fees. A course may instead be free from the outset: original and net fees are zero, with no discount reason, discount grant, or fictitious payment required merely because the configured price is zero. Record fees, received payments, payment allocations, explicit settlements, actual refunds, and linked corrections as distinct operations. Show outstanding registration dues and unallocated credit distinctly. Suggest using available authorized credit first, but let the employee choose explicit allocation or receive a new payment through their normal financial authority; the suggestion does not itself allocate money. Transfers and extra sessions create no automatic fee, allocation, or refund.
- **Financial authorization and currency:** Choose one currency before the first movement and prohibit changing it once movements exist. Branch-scoped accounting views must not expose other-branch movements or infer a private full-center balance through an unauthorized aggregate. Cross-branch credit use requires authority on both branches and financial approval, or existing owner/administrator authority. Debt creates a warning on enrollment or transfer, and the authorized applicant decides whether to proceed. There is no debt-based blocking setting or extra financial-override grant in this phase; waiting also remains allowed. Choosing to proceed does not settle debt or authorize spending another branch's credit.
- **Financial integrity:** Approved entries cannot be edited or deleted; correction uses linked reversing and replacement entries and is distinct from an actual refund. Settlement after withdrawal is manual, not lecture-based proration. Refunds and allocations cannot exceed available amounts. If a corrected payment has already funded registrations, preview received money, affected allocations, available credit, and resulting debt together; an employee authorized for every affected financial action and branch approves linked corrections together with a reason. Preserve the original payment and allocations and the linked correcting entries. Restore the actual resulting unpaid amount to student debt, without presenting an entry correction as an actual cash refund. Use exact currency-safe amount representation, atomic tenant transactions, and database integrity protections for mutually dependent writes. These are proposed implementation safeguards for the approved behavior, not additional accounting products.
- **Retry and concurrency contract:** Repeat submission must not double-create money, allocations, attendance credit, or approved requirement changes. Stale confirmations and conflicting writes must revalidate current state or return a recoverable conflict without partial monetary or academic corruption. Bulk waiting movement may skip newly ineligible students explicitly as specified; do not label that outcome as a complete all-student success.
- **API and UI:** Extend existing authenticated center HTTP operations and Next.js administration. Server responses must supply authorized records, current effective rules, exact preview impact, historical decisions, and clear validation/authorization/conflict outcomes. Reuse the shared shell and tables, Arabic terminology, RTL, accessible confirmation and feedback, theme, and mobile behavior. Endpoint names and physical schema layout are implementation choices, not frozen by this spec. New date, selection, or workflow controls must integrate with the established shared components rather than create separate competing interfaces.
- **Audit and performance:** Academic and financial writes preserve actor, scope, time, reason where required, and safe before/after or source links. Do not record credentials. Ordinary page reads retain the existing maximum of six application SQL queries across central and center connections, with no per-row query growth. Write/approval/generation workflows are measured separately and must remain correct. No unbounded all-center preload is a substitute for scoped reads.

## Testing Decisions

- **Primary acceptance seam, confirmed by the user:** Test complete authenticated employee journeys through the existing browser against the real Laravel HTTP boundary and PostgreSQL center databases. Prefer this highest existing seam for visible behavior rather than adding public testing interfaces or testing internal calculators in isolation. A test succeeds when authorized users can complete the journey and can observe accurate records, history, balances, and denied access.
- **Focused existing HTTP seam:** Use the existing Laravel feature-test boundary for the rule matrix, branch/center isolation, date eligibility, financial atomicity, concurrency, and retries that are too numerous or difficult to synchronize reliably in a browser. This complements the browser journey without introducing a new production seam. Do not mirror implementation or mock away the data/permission boundaries under test.
- **Prior art:** Existing local-acceptance and shared-identity browser suites demonstrate two-center isolation and live protected actions. Branch-management and branch-assignment feature suites demonstrate real per-center PostgreSQL writes, scoped HTTP reads, denied actions, and audit. Existing page-query-budget browser tests measure Laravel requests, including Next.js server-side reads, through local Telescope. Worker-isolation and center-migration tests provide regression coverage when extending tenant migrations or existing background execution.
- **Minimum complete journey:** Create two centers and multiple branches; assign distinct and combined operational roles; create/reuse student and instructor profiles; create independent curricula and a plan version; create a group and schedule; register a late student with a snapshotted fee and a payment; record actual teaching; close attendance; review absence and manually move to waiting; reattach or transfer with a preview; attend an equivalent make-up; review provisional and finalized eligibility, complete the group and explicitly approve selected students; record a settlement or refund through authorized accounting; observe preserved history and unchanged other-center data.
- **Access cases:** Deny direct cross-center identifiers, cross-branch academic/financial records, unsupported actions, sensitive approval without grant, and writes after membership/role revocation on an already-open page. Test disabled and enabled basic-profile search separately, including safe duplicate warnings and a person associated with several branches. A shared login identity must not share center profiles or balances.
- **Attendance and absence cases:** Cover before joining/after departure, unrecorded versus finalized absence, canceled/replacement sessions, own-entry undo versus another employee's edit, denied closed-session correction, recomputation after correction, consecutive versus total thresholds, explicit disabling, rule inheritance, counter reset, and stale bulk selection with explained skips. Assert no automatic move or return.
- **Coverage and completion cases:** Cover late enrollment, identical-version make-up, different-version approval, equal names/numbers/durations without equivalence, duplicate content attendance, eleven-lecture 80% threshold, group-specific added requirements, replacement versus reduced count, transfer between counts, new-version preview, new-registration threshold default, explicit application to current attempts, preservation of approved decisions, exception reasons and true percentage, and per-level course requirements. Assert hours and instructor durations do not weight coverage. An eighth attendance in an open session changes a 7/10 report to a clearly provisional 8/10, but approval is blocked until the supporting session closes; an undo before closure returns the report to 7/10. Verify selective approval on group completion, rejection of stale eligibility, and later approval after a make-up without reopening the completed group. Verify the same study-attempt identity through an equivalent cross-branch transfer while preserving source attendance and fee attribution and prior-branch access restrictions.
- **Financial cases:** Cover shared contact numbers and distinct accounts, snapshotted fees, installments and advance payments, partial allocation across registrations, unallocated credit, denied cross-branch visibility, cross-branch credit approval on both branches, debt warning with the authorized applicant's choice to continue and no financial-override requirement, manual withdrawal settlement versus actual refund, linked reversal/replacement, single-currency lock, insufficient credit, duplicate submission, and concurrent allocation/refund conflicts. A failed action must not leave partial money movements or an unexplained balance. Test the suggestion to use existing credit versus explicitly receiving another payment, with no automatic allocation and continued cross-branch approval enforcement. For fees of 1000, an entered payment of 1000 with 700 allocated and 300 available, corrected to an actual payment of 500, the agreed preview gives 500 allocated, zero available credit, and 500 total unpaid fees: a 200 increase from the earlier 300 debt. Verify the correction and allocation changes succeed together or leave the account unchanged. Test a paid price of 1500 discounted by 200 to 1300, a 100-percent discount to zero with permission and reason, rejection of unauthorized or reasonless discounts and discounts above the price, and a course originally priced at zero without a discount reason or fabricated payment.
- **Presentation and performance:** Verify the main workflows on desktop and narrow screens in Arabic RTL and both themes, including keyboard access, focus restoration, loading, validation, failure, and denied states. Measure ordinary pages cold and warm against the six-query budget through actual backend requests; require real present metrics rather than treating missing counters as zero. Compare representative short and longer authorized lists to detect per-row query growth.
- **Delivery evidence:** Keep repeatable local fixture setup and concrete expected results. The new spec does not claim those tests already exist or have passed. Deployment or production-capacity proof is not inferred from local acceptance or a pushed commit.

## Out of Scope

- Student and instructor portals, their login workflows, and new self-service capabilities.
- Exams, exam attempts, results, certificates, or interpreting academic completion as exam success.
- Instructor compensation, payroll, expenses, general accounting, automated invoicing, subscription billing, per-session fees, monthly tuition models, or automatic withdrawal proration.
- Multiple currencies within one center, exchange conversion, and changing currency after financial history exists.
- Automated student follow-up, selling the next level, messaging campaigns, or external notifications beyond existing supported infrastructure.
- Automatic transfer to waiting, automatic return after correction, automatic fee/refund/allocation on an academic move, and automatic completion of the group.
- Partial student attendance, credited lecture fractions, mandatory subtopic splitting, and hours-weighted completion.
- Custom role builders, cross-center profile merging, cross-database joins, shared live branch curricula, or bypassing branch access through search or account aggregates.
- Production deployment, hosting changes, and capacity claims. This phase's acceptance is local, consistent with the established pilot.

## Further Notes

- This specification synthesizes the current root glossary and locally recorded domain decisions for Phase 3. It preserves the existing pilot and approved frontend foundation; it does not treat earlier unfinished role or completion proposals as settled when later ADRs supersede them.
- This is the approved Phase 3 parent specification in issue #19. The existing pilot provides the administration and testing foundation; academic profiles, curriculum, attendance, and financial operations are new work.
- The browser acceptance and focused HTTP test boundaries and the product decisions in this specification are approved. Implementation should be divided into self-contained vertical slices with explicit acceptance criteria and blocking dependencies.
- Physical table names, endpoint paths, defaults not established by the ADRs, and implementation ordering should be chosen during implementation while preserving these observable contracts. The suggested transfer tables are a conceptual proposal only; only the continuity and historical-attribution behavior is approved. They must not broaden the phase's domain scope.
